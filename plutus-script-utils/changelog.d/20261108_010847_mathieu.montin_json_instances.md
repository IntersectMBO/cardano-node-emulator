### Added

- `ToJSON`/`FromJSON` instances for `Script`, `Validator`, `MintingPolicy`,
  `StakeValidator` and their hashes (`ValidatorHash`, `MintingPolicyHash`,
  `StakeValidatorHash`), together with `ToJSONKey`/`FromJSONKey` instances for
  the hashes. These instances now live in `Plutus.Script.Utils.Scripts`
  alongside their types instead of being defined as orphans in `plutus-ledger`.
- A test suite exercising the JSON round-trips of these script types.

### Fixed

- The forwarding minting policy and stake validator generators in
  `Plutus.Script.Utils.V1.Generators` and `Plutus.Script.Utils.V2.Generators`
  now rely on `PlutusTx.List.any` and PlutusTx's `==` (via `NoImplicitPrelude`)
  instead of their `Prelude`/`Data.Foldable` counterparts, so they compile
  reliably under the PlutusTx plugin.
