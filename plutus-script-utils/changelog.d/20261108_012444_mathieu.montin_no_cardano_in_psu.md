### Removed

- Removed `ToCardanoAddress` and `ToCardanoScriptHash` and the Cardano-backed
  `Versioned` address/hash conversions from `Plutus.Script.Utils`. The
  Cardano-specific equivalents are now in `Ledger.Scripts` and
  `Ledger.Scripts.V1`/`V2`/`V3` (see the `plutus-ledger` changelog).
- `Plutus.Script.Utils.Data` no longer exports `datumHash`, `redeemerHash`,
  `dataHash`, `toCardanoAPIData` or `toMaybeDatumHash`; these are now in
  `Ledger.Data.CardanoAPI` (see the `plutus-ledger` changelog).

### Changed

- `plutus-script-utils` no longer depends on any Cardano library; its only
  blockchain-specific dependencies are Plutus libraries (in addition to
  general-purpose Haskell dependencies).
